Textured schematic preview

Export a placement with #preview3d file.html, then open that file.

Select an unpacked pack folder containing blocks.json, textures/terrain_texture.json and PNG textures. Files stay in this browser; this viewer makes no network requests. Drag to orbit · Shift-drag to pan · Wheel to zoom. Coordinates and orientation already include the placement's rotation and mirror. Models: cubes, slabs, straight stairs and axis-oriented logs. Other shapes use cube placeholders. Connected geometry, biome tint, animated textures, entities and block NBT are not simulated. Missing textures use a pink checkerboard.
